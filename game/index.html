PIXEL VOYAGER

Crack asteroids and moons for minerals and crystals, break planets for gas, level up your ship, and don't fly too close to the suns.

Arrows / WASD or mouse to steer · Space or click to fire, hold for a power shot · P to pause

Pixel Voyager is a tiny space shooter. It needs JavaScript, which this browser isn't running.
